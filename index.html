导言

欢迎使用“天机演象”（以下简称“本应用”）。本应用由演象团队开发与运营。我们非常重视您的个人隐私与数据安全。本隐私政策旨在向您说明我们在您使用本应用时如何收集、使用、存储您的信息，以及您享有的权利。

核心原则：本应用秉持“本地离线计算，数据归属于用户”的隐私设计原则。除苹果官方处理的应用内购买（IAP）交易凭单外，本应用不设外部后端服务器，不会将您的任何传感器、相机画面、位置或户型勘测数据上传至任何云端或第三方服务器。

1. 我们收集的信息及权限使用说明

为了向您提供高精度的专业罗盘堪舆、AR实景勘测及户型分析功能，本应用会在必要时向您申请以下系统权限：

运动与方向传感器 (Motion & Sensors)

使用场景：调用加速度计、陀螺仪与磁力计数据，用于驱动 360° 全息罗盘旋转、双轴太极水准仪倾角检测以及 120 分金刻度瞄准。

存储与保护：所有传感器数据仅在设备内存中实时计算，不落盘，不向外传输。

地理位置信息 (Location Services - When In Use)

使用场景：在您使用罗盘时获取当前经纬度与海拔，用于根据国际地磁模型（WMM）计算当地地磁偏角，以校准“磁北”与“真北”，并推算当地真太阳时与二十四节气流转。

存储与保护：位置信息仅在设备本地离线推算，我们绝不记录或追踪您的行踪轨迹。

相机权限 (Camera)

使用场景：仅在“AR实景天心透视”与“形煞实地立向”功能中用于实时相机预览与空间靶心对齐。

存储与保护：视频流仅在设备屏幕与离屏画板中实时渲染，我们不会录制、上传您的任何实景视频或环境画面。

相册读写权限 (Photo Library)

使用场景：

  • 读取权限：允许您从相册中选择建筑设计图或房型照片，以进行“户型空间中宫立极”诊断；
  • 写入权限：允许您将生成的“福宅天机宣纸长卷”或带水印的实景勘测相片保存至手机相册。

存储与保护：我们仅访问您明确选中的单张图片，绝不检索或读取相册内的其他任何私人照片。

应用内购买与交易数据 (StoreKit & In-App Purchases)

使用场景：用于提供年度会员、大师买断版以及单次测算额度的购买与权益恢复。

存储与保护：所有的支付过程、信用卡或银行账户信息完全由 Apple Inc. 官方加密处理与承载。本应用仅在设备本地利用加密 Keychain 校验 App Store 官方签名的收据凭证，以确认您的会员有效性。

2. 第三方 SDK 与数据共享

无第三方广告追踪：本应用不包含任何第三方广告 SDK（不接入 AdMob、穿山甲等），不收集 IDFA（广告标识符），不进行跨应用用户画像追踪。

不向第三方出售数据：我们绝不向任何数据中介或第三方机构出售、出租或分享用户的任何数据。

3. 数据安全与保存

本应用产生的户型立极草稿、自定义罗盘标记与购买状态均使用 iOS 系统级安全沙盒（Sandbox）与加密 Keychain 存储在您的手机本地。

当您卸载本应用时，存储在应用沙盒内的本地缓存数据将被 iOS 系统一并清除。

本应用不专门面向 4 岁以下的儿童。我们不会故意收集任何儿童的个人可识别信息。

Introduction

Welcome to YanXiang Compass (referred to as “the App”), developed and operated by the YanXiang Team. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Core Commitment: We operate under a “Local-First, Privacy-by-Design” philosophy. The App does not operate proprietary cloud servers. None of your camera frames, motion sensor readings, geolocation data, or architectural floor plans are ever transmitted to any external server.

1. Information Collection & Permissions

To deliver high-precision geomagnetic compass navigation, Augmented Reality (AR) spatial surveys, and architectural Feng Shui analysis, the App requests the following permissions when necessary:

Motion & Direction Sensors (CoreMotion)

Usage: Accesses the device’s accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer to drive the 360° interactive compass dial, dual-axis Taiji level spirit bubble, and degree calculations.

Storage: Sensor outputs are calculated in memory in real time and are never recorded or transmitted externally.

Location Services (When In Use)

Usage: Obtains latitude, longitude, and elevation exclusively to calculate local magnetic declination (True North vs. Magnetic North) based on the World Magnetic Model, as well as solar terms and True Solar Time.

Storage: Geolocation is calculated on-device only; we never track, profile, or retain your location history.

Camera Access

Usage: Used strictly within the AR Spatial Survey mode to project dynamic compass reticles onto real-world sightlines.

Storage: Real-time video feeds are processed locally on the screen. We do not record, store, or upload any photos or videos without your explicit interaction.

Photo Library (Read & Write)

Usage:

  • Read Access: Enables you to import an architectural blueprint or floorplan image for centroid and layout diagnosis.
  • Write Access: Enables you to export and save high-resolution traditional rice-paper scroll reports or watermarked survey snapshots to your Photos.

Storage: The App accesses only the individual photos you explicitly select.

In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

Usage: Manages subscriptions, lifetime unlocks, and credit-based exports.

Storage: All financial transactions, billing details, and payment instruments are processed securely and directly by Apple Inc. The App only verifies cryptographically signed App Store receipts within the device’s local secure Keychain.

2. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers: The App integrates no third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs.

No IDFA Collection: We do not collect Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and do not track you across apps or websites.

No Data Selling: We never sell, lease, or distribute your data to third-party data brokers.

3. Data Retention & Security

All project drafts, compass preferences, and entitlement seals are strictly stored within the iOS application sandbox and local Keychain. Deleting the App removes all locally cached user artifacts from the device.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 4 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
